Professor Mehlhase was hundemuede from riding her drahtesel down Hauptstrasse.
The gebaeck and klimaanlage were just what she needed to recharge.
Her peace was short-lived, however, when an unruly Hund bolted into the 
Biergarten and knocked an entire bowl of Sauerkraut all over her pristine Dirndel.
After a quick cleaning with the staubsauger, she decided she would rather ride home in her Porsche. 
On her way home she spotted a Baeckerei and decided to pull over for a sweet treat after a long day.
Before she could leave, the Hund jumped into the passenger seat and refused to move.
Just as she started her Porsche, a Rammstein song began blasting through the speakers, 
giving her an Ohrwurm so catchy that she couldn't stop headbanging all the way home!
But the Hund was wettbewerbsfaehig and started headbanging with her.
All the headbanging made her very hungry so she decided to stop and get a Doener before arriving home.
Along the way, she decided to stop by a cafe to enjoy a warm slice of Apfelkuchen. 
Her Pantoffelheld had gebaeck waiting for her when she got inside.
Unfortunately, he'd already eaten all the Apfelkuchen, given the crumbs on his Handschuhe.


Translated -
Professor Mehlhase was dog-tired from riding her iron horse (bicycle) down Main Street.
The baked goods and air conditioning were just what she needed to recharge.
Her peace was short-lived, however, when an unruly dog bolted into the
beer garden and knocked an entire bowl of Sauerkraut all over her pristine dirndl.
After a quick cleaning with the vacuum cleaner, she decided she would rather ride home in her Porsche.
On her way home she spotted a bakery and decided to pull over for a sweet treat after a long day.
Before she could leave, the dog jumped into the passenger seat and refused to move.
Just as she started her Porsche, a Rammstein song began blasting through the speakers, 
giving her an earworm so catchy that she couldn't stop headbanging all the way home!
But the dog was competitive and started headbanging with her.
All the headbanging made her very hungry so she decided to stop and get a doner kebab before arriving home.
Along the way, she decided to stop by a cafe to enjoy a warm slice of apple cake.
Her homebody husband had baked goods waiting for her when she got inside.
Unfortunately, he'd already eaten all the apple cake, given the crumbs on his gloves.

Tip - A dirndl is a traditional German Dress.
Rammstein is an industrial metal band
Doener is a sandwich typically with roast meat similar to Gyro/Schwarma.
